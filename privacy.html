Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Homelab Fitbit is a personal app used only by its owner.

  • What it accesses: the owner's own health and activity data from the Google Health API (for example heart rate, steps, SpO2 and activity records), with the owner's consent.
  • How it's used: only to display charts on the owner's private dashboard.
  • Where it's stored: in a database on the owner's own home server. It is not sent to any third party.
  • Sharing: the data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
